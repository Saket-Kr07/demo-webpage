TYPES OF CROPS

What do farmers grow?

Different crops are suited to different climates, soils and farming conditions.

Rice

A major food crop that grows well in warm and water-rich conditions.

Wheat

A widely cultivated cereal used for flour, bread and many other foods.

Vegetables

Vegetable crops include tomato, potato, onion, cabbage and many others.

Fruit crops

Mango, banana, apple and citrus crops are important horticultural products.

FARMING METHODS

Traditional and modern methods

01

Organic farming

Focuses on natural inputs and practices that support soil health.

02

Drip irrigation

Delivers water close to plant roots and can reduce unnecessary water loss.

03

Crop rotation

Growing different crops in sequence can help maintain soil productivity.

04

Precision farming

Uses field data and technology to make more informed farming decisions.

MODERN AGRICULTURE TECHNOLOGIES

Technology in the field

Today's farms can use sensors, drones and digital tools to monitor crops and resources.

Agricultural drones

Drones can help monitor crops from above and support targeted farm operations.

IoT sensors

Soil and weather sensors can provide useful information about field conditions.

Smart irrigation

Sensor-based irrigation can help supply water according to crop and soil conditions.

FARMER TIPS

Simple practices that help

01Check soil moisture before irrigation instead of watering on a fixed schedule.
02Keep basic records of sowing, irrigation, fertilizer and crop health.
03Use weather information when planning irrigation and farm activities.
04Inspect crops regularly so pests and diseases can be noticed early.
05Use farm machinery and agricultural chemicals according to proper instructions.
06Choose technology that is practical and useful for the size of your farm.
